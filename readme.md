# ECN 241: Master Probability & Statistics Jeopardy Lab
**Utica University • Department of Business and Economics**

[![GitHub Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-0C223F?style=flat&logo=github)](https://github.com/securemindorg/ECN-231-Statistics-Jepordy-MidSemester)
[![Course](https://img.shields.io/badge/Course-ECN%20241-E65300?style=flat)]()

An interactive, web-based Jeopardy-style review game designed for **ECN 241: Statistics for Management and Economics**. Built as an active-learning tool to help freshman and sophomore business students review mid-semester probability, descriptive statistics, and decision modeling concepts.

---

## Features

* **Interactive Jeopardy Grid:** 5 core categories with point values ranging from \$100 to \$500.
* **Utica University Branding:** Styled in traditional school colors (**Navy Blue `#0C223F`** and **Orange `#E65300`**).
* **Embedded Statistical Visualizations:** Features dynamically generated vector charts for normal curves and right-skewed distributions directly inside the question modals.
* **Dynamic State Management:** Automatically disables clicked tiles (`—`) to keep track of answered questions during class gameplay.
* **GitHub Integration:** Direct repository link built right into the interface.

---

## Categories & Question Bank

1. **Descriptive Stats:** Population vs. sample, measures of central tendency (mean, median), standard deviation, ordinal data, and distributional shape.
2. **Probability Rules:** Sample spaces, empirical vs. classical probability, addition rules for mutually exclusive events, and multiplication rules for independent events.
3. **Contingency Tables:** Marginal probabilities, joint probabilities, and conditional probability $P(A \mid B)$.
4. **Counting & Combinations:** Permutations, combinations, Excel `=COMBIN()` functions, and fundamental counting principles.
5. **Discrete & Simulation:** Discrete random variables, expected value $E(x)$, Binomial distributions, Poisson distributions, and Monte Carlo simulation modeling in Excel.

## Instructor Classroom Guide

* Project the web app on the classroom screen.
* Divide students into competing teams (e.g., Row 1 vs. Row 2, or Executive Teams).
* Have teams select categories and dollar amounts. Call on the first team to raise their hand.
* Track team scores live on the whiteboard as you reveal answers!
